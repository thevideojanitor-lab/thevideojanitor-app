How it works, what gets sent, and what to build

How it works — the flow

User Actionsubmit, deliver, approve, chat...
→
notifications tableINSERT (already happens)
→
DB WebhookSupabase fires on INSERT
→
send-emailnew Edge Function
→
Resend API→ user's inbox

The key insight: your existing code already inserts into notifications for in-app alerts. A DB Webhook piggybacks on those inserts — zero changes needed to existing Edge Functions.

The 6 emails that matter

🎯
Job Assigned
→ Editor
New edit request — deliver by [date]

📦
Video Delivered
→ Client
Your edit is ready to review ✓
Your editor has delivered the video. Log in to watch it, leave timestamped feedback, or approve it to release payment.
🔄
Revision Requested
→ Editor

Your client has requested changes. Log in to view their timestamped notes and deliver the revision.
✅
Job Approved
→ Editor
Job approved — payout queued
Your client approved the edit. Your payout has been queued and will be processed this week.
💳
Payment Successful
→ Client
Credits added to your account
Your payment was successful. Your account has been credited. Submit your first edit request now.
💬
New Chat Message
→ Client or Editor
[Name] sent you a message
"Hey, just a quick question about the aspect ratio..." — Log in to reply.
⚠️ Rate-limit chat emails — wait 5 min after last message before sending, so rapid-fire chats don't flood inboxes. This is the one trigger that needs extra logic.
