Recommendation: Stone
Why Stone wins
Closest to Notion/Figma references. Neutrals have enough warmth to avoid "generic white SaaS" but stay clean and fast. Won't show staining against your orange accent.
Orange strategy in light
Button fills keep #FF5F15 (vivid orange on white = great contrast). Text/icon usage drops to #C2440E (darker, 4.6:1 on #F7F7F5 ✓ AA). Two orange values, zero confusion.
Toggle implementation
One data-theme="light" attribute on <html>. All CSS variables override inside that selector. Zero component changes needed. localStorage persists the choice.
