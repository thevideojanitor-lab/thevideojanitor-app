What US customers actually trust

US users are trained to look for the Stripe or PayPal checkout experience. An unfamiliar payment screen — even if technically safe — causes hesitation and checkout abandonment. Stripe-powered checkouts have the highest conversion rate in the US market by a significant margin. This alone is a strong argument for the US LLC route.
