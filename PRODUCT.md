# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Content creators (clients) who buy monthly subscriptions and submit short-form video editing requests. They use the app during working hours, often on desktop, to track edit progress and review deliverables. Video editors assigned to jobs use the editor dashboard to pick up briefs and deliver work. Admins manage the ops layer. All users expect the tool to stay out of their way and get things done fast.

## Product Purpose

TheVideoJanitors is a managed short-form video editing platform. Clients submit requests, get matched to a vetted editor automatically, and receive a polished edit within 48 hours. The platform handles the matching, delivery, revision rounds, and payments. Success means: client submits a request and never has to think about logistics — they just receive a great edit.

## Brand Personality

Clean, fast, professional. Direct confidence without corporate stiffness. The tool respects the creator's time and shows it in every interaction — no filler, no fluff, no loading spinners that outlast their welcome.

## Anti-references

- Generic white SaaS dashboards (sterile all-white + blue-accent, 2018-era look)
- Google Material Design (boxy, low-contrast, forms-heavy)
- Corporate enterprise tools (navy + grey, formal table grids, dense sidebars)
- Freelance marketplace aesthetics (crowded, discount-signaling, low trust)

## References

Figma and Notion: warm neutrals, clean hierarchy, approachable without being soft. Linear and Raycast for sharpness of interaction and density done right.

## Design Principles

1. **Status is never a mystery** — the user always knows where their edit stands, without hunting for it.
2. **One screen, one action** — every page answers one question and offers one primary path forward.
3. **Speed is a design choice** — skeletons over spinners, optimistic updates over waiting, instant feedback.
4. **The orange earns every appearance** — the primary accent signals action and value; dilute it and it stops meaning anything.
5. **Both modes are first-class** — light mode is not a desaturated dark mode. It has its own warmth, its own depth, its own identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA (4.5:1 text contrast, 3:1 large text). Light mode should use warm off-whites rather than pure white to reduce harsh brightness. Avoid low-contrast orange-on-light combinations. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Dark mode remains the default; light mode is a user toggle persisted in localStorage.
